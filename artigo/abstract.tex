\undfsetcontent{abstract}{%
This template proposes an organization for an undergraduate final paper in the
form of a scientific or technological article, independent of journal rules. Its
purpose is to provide an editable starting point with a title, author identification,
a single abstract in each selected language, keywords, continuous sections, and
references. The proposal shares the citation, numbering, and bibliography mechanisms
of UnDF-TeX and separates scientific content from the presentation elements required
for institutional submission. Verification includes compilation, reference checking,
and visual examination of the resulting documents. No empirical research findings
are reported, and the presentation choices do not imply approval by any academic
program. The expected outcome is an editable document that supports focused research
communication and allows the same content to be reused for academic submission.
Final adoption depends on checking the requirements of the program, examination
committee, and institutional library.}
\undfsetcontent{keywords}{undergraduate final paper; scientific article; academic writing}
