\undfsetcontent{abstract}{%
	This paper presents a complete undergraduate final paper model aimed at the practical visualization of the structure, writing and formal elements of an academic final project. The final paper itself is adopted as the theme of the text, allowing the document to demonstrate, in a single work, the organization of mandatory and optional elements, the logic of chapters, the use of citations, the insertion of figure, table and equation, as well as the presentation of references, glossary, appendix and annex. The proposal has a didactic and institutional nature, offering students not only an example of the final appearance of the work, but also content that explains the role of each part of the document. In this way, the model seeks to contribute to the understanding of the final paper as an exercise in research, academic writing and standardized presentation of knowledge. It also shows how optional elements may be included without changing the required order of the pre-textual, textual and post-textual parts.}
